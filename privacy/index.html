1. Introduction / who we are & scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Whirl collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Whirl mobile app (the "Service"). Whirl is operated by Mervyn Ong, an individual sole proprietor based in Singapore ("Whirl," "we," "us," or "our"), reachable at privacy@whirl.app.

This policy applies to the Whirl app and this website. It is written to be accurate to exactly what the current version of Whirl does — not a generic template. Where a data practice does not apply to Whirl (for example, advertising or analytics tracking), we say so explicitly rather than leaving it ambiguous.

Whirl is used by parents and guardians (18+) on behalf of the children in their care. Whirl does not offer child accounts.

2. Summary at a glance

The "At a glance" card near the top of this page summarizes the core facts: we collect your account email/identity (via Apple or Google sign-in), your child's approximate age in months, the items you catalog and photos you add, and barcode scan data. We never collect your child's name, your child's date of birth, precise or background location, your contacts, or advertising identifiers. The sections below give the full detail behind that summary.

3. Information we collect

  • Account & identity: your email address (which may be an Apple private-relay address), an optional display name, which provider you signed in with (Apple or Google), an internal user ID, and account timestamps (created, last updated).
  • Child's age: an approximate age in months only, entered by you, used solely to tailor suggestions to an appropriate stage.
  • Inventory data: the items you catalog, the barcode or scan value associated with each item, and any photos you take or select to represent an item (compressed and cached on your device, then uploaded to a private, per-user cloud storage bucket).
  • Consent records: a timestamp and the version of this Privacy Policy you consented to, kept for audit purposes.
  • Device permissions: whether you've granted camera, photo library, and location permission. We explicitly note: even where location permission is granted, the current version of Whirl never reads, uses, transmits, or stores any location coordinate. That permission is primed for a future feature only.
  • No analytics or diagnostic SDKs: Whirl does not bundle any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK that collects behavioral or diagnostic data about you.

5. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and personalize the Service — for example, generating "Today's Pick" suggestions from your own catalog, and tailoring suggestions to your child's approximate age;
  • Store your inventory catalog and item photos so they're available to you across sessions;
  • Authenticate you securely via Apple or Google sign-in;
  • Meet our legal and compliance obligations, including maintaining consent records.

We do not use your information for advertising, ad targeting, or profiling of any kind.

6. Legal bases & parental consent

Whirl is used by a parent or guardian on behalf of their household, not directly by a child. Where children's data protection frameworks (such as GDPR-K or Singapore's PDPA) require parental consent for processing information relating to a child, that consent is captured from you, the account holder, at onboarding — recorded as a timestamp plus the policy version you agreed to (see Section 3). By creating and using a Whirl account, you are confirming that you are the parent or guardian consenting on behalf of your household for the limited child-related data described in this policy (your child's approximate age in months).

You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (Section 10), which removes the underlying data this consent covers.

Our legal bases for processing (in GDPR terms) are: performance of a contract (providing the Service you signed up for), consent (for the limited child-age data and permissions described above), and legitimate interest / legal obligation (for security, fraud prevention, and compliance recordkeeping).

7. How your information is stored & who processes it

We use a small number of service providers ("processors") to operate Whirl, and we only share what each one needs to do its job:

  • Supabase (Postgres database and file storage, hosted in the Singapore region) — our primary processor, storing your account record, inventory data, and item photos. Item photos are held in a private, per-user storage bucket protected by row-level security, meaning only your authenticated account can read or write your own photos.
  • Apple and Google — used solely to authenticate you when you sign in; we receive only the identity information those providers share as part of sign-in (such as your email address).
  • Open Library, Google Books, and UPCitemdb — external lookup APIs used to look up item details from a barcode you scan. These lookups send only the barcode or identifier itself — never your name, email, account ID, or any other personal data. (BoardGameGeek (BGG) is planned as a future lookup source for board games specifically, but is not yet active; this policy will be updated when that lookup goes live.)

We retain your account and inventory data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, the associated data is deleted as described in Section 10. Photos cached locally on your device for offline viewing are cleared when the app or account is removed from that device.

10. Your rights & how to exercise them

Access & correction: you can view and edit your account details and inventory directly in the app at any time.

Erasure: go to Settings → Delete Account in the app. This triggers our account-deletion process, which deletes your authentication record, cascades deletion of your profile and inventory rows in our database, and purges your stored item photos from cloud storage. This action is permanent and irreversible — there is no recovery period or backup restore once it completes.

Singapore PDPA: you have the right to request access to, and correction of, your personal data, and to withdraw consent for its collection, use, or disclosure (subject to legal or contractual restrictions). Requests can be directed to privacy@whirl.app, which serves as our contact point for data protection matters.

GDPR (EU/UK users): you have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of your personal data, to receive a copy of your data in a portable format, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Parental rights over child-related data: because the only child-related data Whirl holds is an approximate age in months tied to your own account, reviewing or deleting it is achieved by editing that field in-app or deleting your account entirely.

For any request you'd rather not handle directly in-app, email privacy@whirl.app and we will respond within a reasonable time.

11. Children's privacy

Whirl is built around a strict data-minimization principle when it comes to children: parents are the account holders, and children do not have accounts of their own. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The only child-related information Whirl ever stores is an approximate age in months, entered by the parent or guardian, and used solely to tailor activity suggestions.

Whirl does not collect a child's name, date of birth, photo, or any other directly identifying information about a child. This is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight.

COPPA (US): Whirl does not collect personal information from children under 13; the app is intended for use by the parent or guardian, who is the account holder and sole user of the app's interface.

GDPR-K (EU) / Article 8: where processing relates to a child, consent is obtained from the parent or guardian at account creation, as described in Section 6.

Singapore PDPA: the same parental-consent model applies, consistent with PDPA guidance on data belonging to minors.

If you believe Whirl has inadvertently collected information about a child beyond what is described in this policy, please contact us immediately at privacy@whirl.app so we can investigate and delete it.

12. Security

We apply the following safeguards to protect your information:

  • Authentication sessions are stored in your device's secure Keychain, not in plain text;
  • Database access is protected by row-level security (RLS) policies, so an account can only read or write its own data;
  • Item photos are held in a private storage bucket, scoped per-user, not publicly accessible;
  • Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our backend;
  • Access to backend infrastructure is limited to what each service and process needs (least-privilege access).

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we design Whirl to minimize what could be exposed even in the event of an incident, by minimizing what we collect in the first place.

13. International data transfers

Whirl's data is hosted with Supabase in the Singapore region. If you are located in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or elsewhere outside Singapore, using Whirl involves transferring your information to Singapore for processing and storage. Where such a transfer occurs, we apply appropriate safeguards to protect your information consistent with applicable data protection law. We have not (as a small, early-stage operator) put in place a specific formal mechanism such as Standard Contractual Clauses at this time, and we are transparent about that here rather than overstating our current compliance posture; if this changes, we will update this policy accordingly.
