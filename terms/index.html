4. What the service does

Whirl lets you build a personal catalog of items you own — board games, craft supplies, toys, books, and similar — by scanning a barcode or taking a photo. Whirl attempts to identify scanned items automatically using barcode-lookup services, and lets you add or correct details by hand. From that catalog, Whirl's "Today's Pick" feature randomly surfaces one item as a suggestion for something to do today, with the option to re-roll for another suggestion.

Whirl is an early-stage, actively evolving product. Features, screens, and the specific way suggestions are generated may change, be added, or be removed over time as we improve the Service. We will use reasonable efforts to keep this document current, but the app itself is the authoritative description of what is currently available.
